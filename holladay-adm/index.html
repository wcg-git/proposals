03 · What We Deliver

The work, in concrete terms.

Four phases take the weekend from run of show to finished files.

01

Pre production

A short planning call the week of the event to lock the run of show, speaker list, camera positions at Park24, and the bus tour logistics. We coordinate audio for every speaker and panel format in advance, whether the session is slides or conversational, so the room never waits on us.

02

Friday, September 18. Full program day at Park24

A four person crew on site from morning kickoff through the Park24 tour. Two roaming cameras cover the room, the breaks, the lunch fireside chat, and the tour. A third stationary camera records every educational session from a locked position with broadcast grade audio on each speaker. Our photographer works the full day alongside.

03

Saturday, September 19. Property tour through noon

Video and photography ride the bus from Park24 to Shelby House, The Opaline, and Stone Bridge Lofts. We capture the properties, the participant reactions, and the conversations that happen between stops, then wrap at noon.

04

Edit and delivery

One long form recap film of the weekend, color graded, mixed, and titled in the ADM look. A set of vertical social clips cut from the best moments. Every Friday session cleaned, synced, and delivered as its own course ready file. A curated, edited photo library delivered through a shared folder with clear naming.
