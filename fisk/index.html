02 · The Story

Students first. Not a room full of people speaking from the front.

Pulled straight from Veronica's brief. The finished material should communicate that college students from across the South came together in Nashville to learn how public policy works, understand how power and government institutions shape policy outcomes, explore election administration, and develop practical skills they can take back home. Here is the shot plan we will build the 2 days around.

The Environment

Arrival and place

Exterior and location shots of Scarritt Bennett. Workshop signage and PSI, Brennan, Fisk, and Howard branding. Registration and check in. Name badges and materials. Students arriving, greeting each other, and meeting presenters. Wide shots of the room.

The Sessions

Learning, in the room

45 to 60 seconds of each presenter speaking. Wide shots of presenter and students together. Students listening, reacting, and asking questions. Presenters interacting directly with students. Applause, laughter, and the moments in between.

The Simulations

Small groups, real decisions

Students huddled and working around tables. Debates. Writing on worksheets and materials. Comparing ideas and presenting to teammates. Making decisions as a group. The policy presentations on Friday afternoon.

The Voices

Short interviews and portraits

Speakers, panelists, and attendees at the interview and portrait station, answering PSI's question list in 30 to 60 second clips. Natural sound from sessions and simulations woven through the recap.

04 · What We Deliver

The work, in concrete terms.

Four phases take the coverage from planning call to finished files.

01

Planning and venue walk through

We open with a planning call to lock the run of show against the agenda, the presenter list, PSI's interview question list, and who asks the questions on camera. Then we walk the Raintree room at Scarritt Bennett Center ahead of October 1 to plan camera positions, the interview and portrait station location, lighting, power, and where photography moves during sessions and simulations.

02

Two days on site

Two cameras and a dedicated photographer from registration on Thursday through the wrap up on Friday. One camera holds the presenter and the room with a wireless microphone on the presenter for clean session clips. The second camera works the students: reactions, questions, table work, and the simulations. The interview and portrait station runs during registration, breaks, lunch, and the dinner break, staffed from the crew, so sessions stay covered.

03

Edit and story shaping

We cut a recap film that moves the way the 2 days did, led by the students and carried by natural sound and interview voices. Reels are cut vertical with captions. Interview clips are cut for horizontal and vertical. Photography is culled, color corrected, and delivered as a finished gallery. Your team reviews online and we refine through revision rounds.

04

Delivery for every channel

Everything arrives through a shared folder with clear naming. Recap masters for the PSI website and screen, reels and clips formatted for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn, and a photo gallery ready for press, social, reports, and partner use. Masters are archived for 12 months.

09 · The Interview and Portrait Station

The voices that carry the story.

You asked for a speaker, panelist, and attendee portrait and interview station on site. It is included in the engagement at no additional cost. Here is how it runs. We set a lit backdrop in a quiet corner of the Raintree room or an adjacent space, confirmed on the walk through. The station opens during registration, breaks, lunch, and the dinner break, so it never pulls a camera away from a session or a simulation. Katie or another PSI colleague can bring students and presenters over between blocks.

On the questions. Send us PSI's list ahead of the planning call. We are glad to ask them from behind the camera so the answers sound like a conversation, or one of your team can sit in as the interviewer if you would rather the questions come from PSI. Either way, each clip is cut to 30 to 60 seconds for horizontal and vertical use, and the best answers also find their way into the recap.
