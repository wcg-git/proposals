01 · The Opportunity

The conversation will happen once. The record should last.

Dr. Franklin, thank you for the note and the call, and please thank the Tennessee NAACP for the introduction.

A student conference on democracy and justice in the South, hosted by the John Lewis Center in partnership with the Brennan Center for Justice, is exactly the kind of room that deserves a record. Panels of experts, questions from students, and 2 full days of conversation at Scarritt Bennett Center. Our job is to capture it cleanly, stay out of the way, and hand you a recap, reels, and photography that your team and your partners can put to work the week after.

03 · What We Deliver

The work, in concrete terms.

Four phases take the coverage from planning call to finished files.

01

Planning and venue walk through

We open with a planning call to lock the run of show, the speaker list, and what matters most to you and the Brennan Center on the back end. Then we walk Scarritt Bennett Center ahead of October 1 to plan camera positions, lighting, power, and where photography moves during sessions. We also confirm the audio plan with the venue and your AV contact, which has its own section below.

02

Two days on site

Camera coverage of every session on the program. At the middle tier and above a second camera lets panel conversations cut between the speakers and the room. A dedicated photographer works the full program both days. Our crew arrives ahead of doors, stays through the final session, and stays out of the way of the conversation.

03

Edit and story shaping

We cut a recap film that moves the way the 2 days did, built from the strongest moments across the panels, the students, and the partners in the room. Reels are cut vertical with captions and titles. Photography is culled, color corrected, and delivered as a finished gallery. Your team reviews online and we refine through revision rounds.

04

Delivery for every channel

Everything arrives through a shared folder with clear naming. Recap masters for web and screen, reels formatted for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn, and a photo gallery ready for press, social, reports, and the Brennan Center. Masters are archived for 12 months.

08 · Separate · The Audio Question

Capturing a panel means capturing the sound.

If Scarritt Bennett Center or your AV vendor is running microphones through a house system, we take a clean feed from the board and every tier above covers it. If there is no house system, or no feed is available to us, we bring our own. We have kept this separate on purpose so the tiers stay clean and you only pay for it if the venue needs it.

One detail worth flagging now. Questions from the audience only end up on the recording if they reach a microphone. If the room will have a handheld for student questions, a house feed covers it. If not, the package here covers that too. Please confirm your AV setup on the planning call, and if you are not sure, we will ask the venue directly.

Independent Audio Package · $1,800 · Both days

Added to any tier, only if there is no house AV feed
