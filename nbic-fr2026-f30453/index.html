What changed, and why

Every change below responds to a pattern in the current registration data. Figures are in the accompanying proposal.

  1. County is asked first, not last. Roughly one in five registrants lives outside the funded counties. Asking first identifies them before they take a limited seat, and routes them to a waitlist instead of a hard rejection.
  2. One session per registration. Seat-level attendance is roughly three times stronger for people who pick a single session than for those who pick several. Multi-select was manufacturing seats nobody intended to use.
  3. A second session is offered after confirmation, not before. Multi-session registrants still supply most unique attendees, so the cross-sell is preserved deliberately. It just happens after one real commitment exists.
  4. Seats are shown as scarce, because they are. Free plus unlimited reads as low value. A visible seat count gives the registration a cost.
  5. An explicit commitment checkbox and a one-click reschedule. Turns a passive sign-up into a stated intention, and gives a no-show an honest exit that frees the seat.
  6. A certificate that requires attendance. Creates a reason to show up that "free and optional" does not provide.
  7. SMS reminders on by default. The add-on is already active on the account and pending carrier registration.
