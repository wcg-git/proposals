02 · The Evening

Two rooms. Two energies. One story.

The evening moves in two acts, and the coverage is built to match. Here is the shot plan for October 22.

Act One · The Main Hall

The gathering

Arrivals, the Ambassador's entrance, greetings, handshakes, and the conversations that happen over drinks. A roaming camera and photographer work the room so the energy reads on screen, with partner signage and the NMAAM setting captured on purpose.

The Transition

Into the theater

The crowd moving from the hall into the theater, seats filling, the moment before the lights settle. Small transitions like this are what make a recap feel like an evening instead of a slideshow.

Act Two · The Theater

Panel and fireside chat

A wide and a tight camera locked on the stage, audio pulled from the house board with a backup recorder, and the roaming camera catching audience reactions, laughter, and applause. Similar in format to Future Way Forward, which we covered in the same building.

The Voices

Portraits and short interviews

In the top tier, a lit portrait and interview station near the hall gives the Ambassador, panelists, and guests a place to share a thought in 30 to 60 seconds. Those voices carry the recap and the social cuts.

03 · What We Deliver

The work, in concrete terms.

Four phases take the evening from planning call to finished files.

01

Planning and run of show

We open with a planning call to lock the run of show, the panel and fireside lineup, the Ambassador's arrival timing, and any protocol or press guidance from the Embassy. We coordinate directly with the NMAAM AV team, who run the microphones and house system, on the board feed, stage lighting, screen content, and camera positions in the theater.

02

The evening on site

Crew arrives well before doors to set the theater and test audio. The roaming camera and photographer cover the main hall gathering. When the room moves into the theater, the stage cameras roll on the full panel and fireside chat while the roaming camera stays on the audience.

03

Edit and story shaping

We cut the recap to move the way the evening did, from the warmth of the hall to the weight of the conversations. The panel and fireside chat are edited as a full multi camera program with titles and lower thirds. Photography is culled and color corrected. Your team reviews online and we refine through revision rounds.

04

Delivery for every channel

Everything arrives in a shared folder with clear naming. Recap and program masters for web and screen, vertical reels captioned for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn, and a photo gallery ready for press, the Embassy, and partner use. Masters are archived for 12 months.

08 · What We Need From You

A few answers turn the model into a fixed quote.

Send whatever you have, even a rough draft. These are the questions that decide crew, hours, and deliverables.

  • The itinerary. Each stop on October 21, 23, and 24 with times, locations, and who will be there. Even a draft tells us whether a day needs a full crew, a photographer, or nothing at all.
  • Which moments matter most. The key moments you want documented, and which are public, private, or press only. Some diplomatic meetings are photo only or closed to cameras, and we plan around that.
  • What the content is for. Posting live during the visit, a film for the Embassy and partners afterward, an archive, or all three. That decides whether you need same day turnaround or a visit film.
  • Slides or a static banner. Whether the panel and fireside chat will use slides on the theater screen or a static event banner. If there are slides, please send the files ahead of the event so the program edit cuts to clean slides instead of a filmed screen. If it is a banner, we frame the wide shot to keep your branding in view.
  • Livestream. Whether the conversations should stream live or feed overflow screens. If yes, The Moment tier covers it.
  • Embassy contact. The Ambassador's communications or protocol contact, so we can confirm arrival timing, credentials, and any restrictions on filming or photography.
  • Usage. Who will publish the finished work: CoRo, The Conscious Group, the Embassy, partners, or all of the above.
