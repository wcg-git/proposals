03 · Your List

The seven things you named, and where each one lives.

Your note listed the anticipated work. This is the honest answer to each line, pointed at the piece above that proves it.

01

Executive and thought leadership interviews

R.E.D. Academy is built on them. Controlled lighting, two camera coverage, audio that survives a room with air handling in it, and a subject who forgets the camera is there. Most of what separates a good interview day from a bad one happens before the first question, and that is the part we run.

02

Long form video content

Titan's House and Holladay Ventures are both long form pieces carried by structure rather than pace. When 30|90 brings the script and the arc, we shoot to it. When the story is still forming on location, we capture for the cut you have not written yet.

03

Short form vertical for social

Vertical is a capture decision, not a crop decision. On our client days we frame and shoot dedicated vertical alongside the primary coverage so the social cuts are native rather than salvaged. Your team owns the edit and the posting calendar; we make sure the media supports it.

04

On location at properties, developments and events

This is our most repeated day. Holladay Ventures is an ongoing development storytelling client. The Titans stadium project runs through Turner Construction with Polk and Associates on the team. Active sites, badging, safety briefings, and the reality that the best light and the best access rarely arrive at the same hour.

05

Conferences and behind the scenes

NPEF Hall of Fame, the Eastpoint Neighborhood Kickoff, and TECA Graduation are all single day events with no second take. Multi camera where the program demands it, a stationary session record where the client wants the content afterward, and roaming coverage for the room.

06

B roll and establishing footage

Every piece above is carried by it. We shoot B roll as a deliverable rather than as leftovers, and hand it over organized and labeled so your editors are not hunting through a card dump at eleven at night.

07

Drone and aerial

Available, flown by a licensed operator, and planned against airspace before the shoot date rather than on it. Nashville has airspace that will end a flight day if nobody checked. We check.

06 · On Numbers

You asked for rates. Here is why they are not on this page.

A rate card would be the fastest way to answer your note and the slowest way to get you a number you could actually use. Half day and full day pricing only holds when the day is known. Two interviews in a controlled room and a full day on an active construction site with badging, a safety escort, aerial, and a second operator are not the same day, and quoting them at the same number would cheat one of us.

We are not a videographer for hire with a posted rate. We are a production company that scopes an engagement, sources the right crew and gear for it, and prices it against what the work actually takes. That takes one conversation, not a spreadsheet.

So here is the trade. Give us thirty minutes on the engagement you are scoping, or send over the brief when the client signs. You will have real numbers within two business days, broken out the way you asked, with crew, gear, aerial, and travel itemized so you can build your own margin on top with confidence. If the relationship becomes ongoing, we will build a standing structure for it so you are not re quoting every shoot.

Worth flagging early

  • Milestone days like a groundbreaking, a ribbon cutting, or a conference keynote get scoped separately from any ongoing arrangement, because they carry their own crew load
  • Aerial requires airspace clearance planned in advance, so tell us as early as you can
  • We hold dates on a signed scope, and Nashville books out fast in the fall
