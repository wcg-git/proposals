05 · How We Work Together

Rhythm, so nothing goes quiet.

Every Friday

A short written update

Five lines. What went live, what came in, what is next. No meeting required and nobody has to chase us for a status.

Every month

Report and working session

What arrived, what it cost, what changes next month. Numbers first. If something is not working we will say so before you ask.

Day to day

Slack, if you want it

Faster than email when a site opens up or a schedule shifts. If your team would rather stay on email, that works too. Tell us which and we will use it.

On site

We work around the crew

Filming never adds a day to a project. Our crew follows your site protocols and carries its own insurance. If a shot would slow the work, we lose the shot.

One thing we will ask of you

When inquiries start arriving they need to be answered quickly. We can build something that performs and still have the quarter look like a failure because calls sat unreturned for three days. Call tracking will show both of us what came in and whether it was picked up. We would rather raise that now than in month two.
