Boyd's Commentary Advertising: What Week One Taught Us,
and the Two Changes That Unlock It

Prepared for Finance and Shipping review · July 27, 2026 · Walton Creative Group

Executive Summary

Our new Meta ads are successfully bringing buyers to rhboyd.com. The Worded Hymnal campaign produced 21 orders in week one. The Boyd's Commentary campaign produced the same strong traffic and add-to-carts, but shoppers abandon at one exact step: the shipping quote. A $25.99 book showing $14 to $18 shipping becomes a $40+ purchase, and single-copy buyers walk away. Two changes fix this: a book-rate shipping option for single-copy orders, and a Commentary + Journal bundle that makes the total feel right. Meanwhile, we redirect ad targeting toward the buyers already proving they convert: churches ordering in bulk.

The product is not the problem. Boyd's Commentary sold roughly 100 copies this same week through direct and email bulk orders, where shipping spreads across many books. Only the single-copy retail buyer arriving from ads is priced out. This matches the May cart-abandonment audit, which found checkout shipping to be the store's largest recovery opportunity (559 abandoned carts, $57K in 90 days).

The Strategy

Two lanes, each built on what the data proved

LANE 1 · CHURCH & BULK

Advertise to the buyer who already buys

Redirect Commentary prospecting toward pastors, Sunday School directors, and ministry leaders, using assets we already hold:

  • The R.H. Boyd church mailing list (12,600 contacts) as a direct Meta audience
  • A lookalike audience of 2.2M people modeled on that list
  • Copy reframed from personal devotion to equipping a classroom: "Outfit every teacher and student for the new Sunday School year"

Journal's role: the incentive, not the hero. "Free Bible Study Journal with Commentary orders of 5 or more" via a standard Shopify discount. No new product setup required.

LANE 2 · RETAIL BUNDLE

Make the single-buyer total feel right

Sell the Commentary and Journal together at one bundle price, on one page, added to cart in one click:

  • Bundle price $39.99 (vs $45.98 separately) makes a $12–15 shipping quote proportionally acceptable
  • One-click "Get Both" button adds both items to cart automatically. Shopify supports this natively; no custom development
  • Honors Dr. Boyd's direction to advertise both products together

Requires: Finance approval of the bundle price, and a Shopify page Emmanuel's team can publish from the mockup below.

How the one-click cart works

STEP 1

Shopper clicks the ad and lands on the bundle page (a standard Shopify page, pixel tracking intact).

STEP 2

"Get Both" uses a Shopify cart permalink: both items enter the cart in one click, discount code applied automatically.

STEP 3

Checkout shows $39.99 plus shipping. The total now reads as fair, and the buyer completes.

No custom development, no app subscriptions. The permalink and discount are native Shopify features. A dedicated bundle product with its own inventory sync can follow later as the permanent version.

The Asks

For the Shipping Department

Enable a book-rate option for single-book retail orders:

  • USPS Media Mail (books qualify; typically about $5 for one book) or a flat $4.99 book rate
  • Applies to single-copy and small retail orders; bulk orders keep current carriers
Impact: this week alone, 11 Commentary checkouts died at the shipping quote. The May audit projected roughly $18K per 90 days recoverable storewide.

For Finance

Approve two pricing actions:

  • Bundle price of $39.99 for Commentary + Journal ($5.99 off combined retail)
  • A Buy X Get Y discount: one free Bible Study Journal on Commentary orders of 5 or more copies
Impact: unlocks a stalled ad campaign already producing 30 add-to-carts per week, and arms the church lane with a compelling bulk offer.

With these approved, Walton Creative Group can have the church-lane ads live within two business days and the bundle page ready for Emmanuel's team the same week. The Worded Hymnal campaign proves the audience buys when the checkout math is fair; these changes give Boyd's Commentary the same fair fight.
