01 · The Opportunity

The stadium will speak for itself. The impact needs a storyteller.

Herbert, Tony, thank you for the conversation. It was clear within minutes that this project already has its story. It just has not been told on screen yet.

When the new stadium opens, every camera in the country will point at the building. What they will not see is what happened around it for three years. A training camp that turned neighbors into craft professionals. Small businesses that grew up inside a landmark project. Students who stood on the site and saw a future. That is the story the Titans, the Tennessee Builders Alliance, and Turner get to own, and it deserves the same standard of production as the building itself.

Our goal is simple. When a Turner or Titans executive steps up to speak, the film that plays before them should do the heavy lifting. And anyone who finds these stories afterward should understand exactly what this project meant to Nashville.

05 · What We Deliver

The work, in concrete terms.

Four phases take this from ideation to launch.

01

Story mapping and pre production

We sit down with your team, and with Johari and the Titans at the right moment, to map the story before anything is recorded. Who speaks, what each voice carries, and how the pieces fit. Tony's interview list becomes a locked roster, and we request archival footage from the Titans library so the edit starts with the full history in hand.

02

Interview and site production

Multi camera interview setups with cinema lighting and broadcast grade audio, in the spaces Turner designates on site or in conference settings. Two dedicated interview days carry the roster, a half day catches the voices in town for the conference, and supporting footage is captured around every setup. Two dedicated b roll days complete the picture, planned where they count most.

03

Edit, archive weave, and story shaping

Every film gets its own arc. New interviews are woven with archival groundbreaking and graduation footage so the story spans the full life of the project. Color grade, audio mix, and clean titles in a look that respects the Titans, TBA, and Turner brands. Your team reviews online and we refine through revision rounds.

04

Delivery for every screen and stage

Masters arrive ready for the premiere screen, the boardroom, the website, and social channels. Files come through a shared folder with clear naming, organized as a catalog your teams can pull from for years. We archive the masters for twelve months after delivery.

06 · Timeline

The calendar is the strategy.

Three windows will not come back. The annual meeting week, the trailers on site, and the final graduation. We plan around them and the rest falls into place, with the finished films in hand well before the stadium opens.

Late July

Week of August 17
Annual meeting interview block. Turner's community and citizenship professionals from across the country are in Nashville. We capture executives and the national story in one concentrated window.
August to October
Production window. Two dedicated interview days plus the conference week half day, supporting footage around every setup, and two b roll days across the TC2 trailers and the site.
Before Thanksgiving
The final cohort wraps and the all cohort celebration honors graduates from the full program. If the calendar allows, a b roll day lands here, capturing the moment the story builds toward.
December to January
Edit and review. New capture woven with the archive, revision rounds with your team and the Titans.
Near turnover
The gathering. Graduates, business owners, and partners walk the finished building together, on camera if scheduling allows. It may be the most powerful scene in the film.
Early February 2027
Final masters delivered. Every film finished, formatted, and in your hands ahead of turnover.
Late Feb / Early March
The stadium opens. The story launches with it.

11 · Optional · Milestone Events

The celebration days deserve their own crew.

The films above tell the story. The big days along the way, the all cohort graduation celebration, the wrap up gathering in the finished building, annual meeting gatherings, and the opening itself, are each their own production with their own crew and plan.
