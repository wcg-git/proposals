01 · What We Heard

Construction leads. The floors open the door.

The proposal put athletic surfaces out front because that is where the search demand was easiest to prove. You corrected that, and the correction is right. Henderick is a construction and facilities firm. Gym floors are the most photogenic thing you do, which makes them an excellent way to get a facility director to stop scrolling, but they are not the business.

So the program runs on two tracks. Athletic surfaces earn the attention. Construction, renovation, paving, grading, ADA work, and facilities management carry the search terms, the landing pages, and the conversation that follows. A buyer who finds you because of a gym floor should understand within thirty seconds that you build and maintain the whole building.

02 · What We Measure

Let us agree on the scoreboard before we start.

You are buying three months to find out whether this produces work. That is a fair test and we want to pass it on the merits. So it matters that we agree now on what can honestly be proven in ninety days, rather than discovering a disagreement in October.

Here is the thing that shapes everything. Commercial construction does not close quickly. In a survey of more than a thousand commercial sales leaders, 44 percent reported sales cycles of four months or longer and 23 percent reported cycles beyond twelve months. A lead created in September may sign in February. That is normal for your industry and it is not a marketing problem.

What we will prove by day 90

  • Qualified inquiries arriving, counted and attributed to a source
  • What an inquiry costs, measured against published category benchmarks
  • Which service lines actually pull, and which do not
  • Pipeline created. Named opportunities, quotes issued, site visits booked
  • A content library you own outright and can keep using
  • Search presence you did not have, with data on what more would buy

What ninety days cannot prove

  • Full year return, because most of the revenue has not closed yet
  • Closed jobs at volume, for the cycle reasons above
  • Whether a slower building channel like search rankings has matured
  • Seasonal patterns, since we will have seen one quarter of a calendar that runs on twelve

The definition we are proposing

At day ninety, success means a working lead engine with attributed inquiries at a known cost, a named pipeline you can point at, and a library that keeps producing after the invoice stops. If that is not the right bar, tell us at the onboarding meeting and we will set a different one together. What we do not want is two different scoreboards discovered in month three.

03 · The Roadmap

Ninety days, week by week.

Two capture sessions a month, six across the term. A session is one subject carried all the way through: planning it, filming it, cutting it, publishing it. Two sessions can share a day in the field so your crews are interrupted once, not twice.

Week 1

Access, accounts, and the first shoot date

The least glamorous week and the one everything else depends on.

  • Social accounts audited, claimed where they exist, built where they do not
  • Google Business Profile submitted for verification, which can take up to two weeks, so it starts first
  • Ad accounts created under Henderick ownership
  • Production schedule reviewed so filming lands on work already happening
  • Written permissions requested for any school or government site
Weeks 2 and 3

First capture, and search goes live

Two sessions. Waddell on camera, and an active construction or facilities site.

  • Founder interview. Thirty four years, and what Henderick holds itself to
  • First site shoot, construction forward rather than floors
  • Google Ads built and launched with call tracking attached
  • Two landing pages, construction and facilities, and athletic surfaces
  • Organic posting begins on every platform
Weeks 4 to 6

Volume and the first read

Enough data arrives to start steering rather than guessing.

  • Sessions three and four, including the superintendents and their crews on camera
  • First search term report. Wasteful terms cut, productive ones widened
  • Month one performance report and working session
  • Multi state search research begins in the background
Weeks 7 to 9

Paid social enters with a real library

By now there is proven material rather than guesses about what works.

  • Meta launches using the organic pieces that already earned attention
  • Retargeting to people who visited but did not call
  • Session five, the visual hero piece
  • Month two report. Cost per inquiry now has enough history to be real
Weeks 10 to 12

The case study, and the decision

The asset that outlives the term, and an honest look at the numbers.

  • Session six. One named project start to finish, with a client interview if permission allows
  • Full performance review across the term
  • Three state groundwork presented, ready to launch
  • Day 75 renewal conversation, deliberately before the term ends

05 · Alabama, Mississippi, Arkansas

We looked into it. They are not the same door.

You mentioned wanting to serve all three. Before planning any marketing there, we looked at how a Tennessee contractor actually gets to work in each one, because there is no point generating demand in a state where the door is closed. The differences are larger than we expected, and one state is clearly friendlier to your position.

The good news first. All three have reciprocity agreements with Tennessee, which is better than the usual answer and means no starting from zero on examinations.

What this means for the marketing order

Arkansas is the one worth building toward first. It is the only one of the three where a cooperative contract is a named legal route into school construction, which means visibility and a documented record can actually get Henderick onto a list. In the other two, the door is a sealed bid, and no amount of marketing opens a sealed bid. Separately, private work below each threshold needs no license at all, which is a nearer term opening than full licensure and fits the private market strategy exactly.

Inside the ninety days we do groundwork only. Search research for all three, page structure that can take a state page, and service areas configured. No advertising spend outside Tennessee, because splitting a modest budget across four markets would prove nothing about any of them. Expansion launches when you decide to continue.

06 · How We Work Together

Rhythm, so nothing goes quiet.

Every Friday

A short written update

Five lines. What went live, what came in, what is next. No meeting required and no chasing us for a status.

Every month

Report and working session

What arrived, what it cost, what changes next month. Numbers first, opinions second.

On site

We work around the crew

Filming never adds a day to a project. Our crew follows your site protocols and carries its own insurance. If a shot would slow the work, we lose the shot.

Day 75

The renewal conversation

Deliberately before the term runs out, with the full picture and a clear recommendation, so the decision is never made under time pressure.

One thing we will ask of you

When inquiries start arriving, they need to be answered quickly. We can build a lead engine that performs and still have the quarter look like a failure because calls sat unreturned for three days. Call tracking will show both of us what came in and whether it was picked up. We raise this now rather than in month two.
